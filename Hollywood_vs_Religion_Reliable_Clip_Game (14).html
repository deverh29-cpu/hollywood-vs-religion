⚠️ When Movies Portray Religion Hurtfully

The Last Temptation of Christ (1988): A fictionalized portrayal of Jesus that was considered deeply offensive or disrespectful by some Christian audiences.

Religulous (2008): A satirical documentary that strongly criticizes and mocks religious beliefs and institutions. Some viewers see it as comedy and criticism, while others find its treatment of religion hurtful or disrespectful.

These are examples of controversial portrayals, not statements that everyone in a religion will find them offensive.
